Not every pelvic floor problem is weakness. Some men have a floor that stays too tight. More squeezing can then make urgency, pain or tension worse.

Choose relaxation when you have pelvic pain, pain with sitting, or when squeezes leave you more tense. Focus on lengthening, not lifting.

Try:

- Slow belly breaths: on the in-breath, let the floor soften and descend
- Reverse Kegel: gently open and drop, without pushing down hard
- Child's pose or supported back-lying: breathe and let go for a few minutes

If pain continues, or exercises hurt, see a doctor or pelvic floor physiotherapist. This app does not diagnose.
