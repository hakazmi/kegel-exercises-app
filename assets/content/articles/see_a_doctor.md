This app guides exercises. It does not diagnose conditions or replace clinical care.

See a doctor or pelvic floor physiotherapist if:

- You have pain at rest, with squeezes, or with sexual activity
- You have a catheter in place (skip strengthening until it is removed)
- Leakage or urgency is new, sudden or severe
- You are unsure you have found the right muscles
- After prostate surgery, control is not improving over months as your team expected
- A check-in shows no change after about eight weeks of steady practice

Ask for referral to a continence or pelvic floor physiotherapist when you need hands-on feedback.
