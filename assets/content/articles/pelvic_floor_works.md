In men, the pelvic floor runs from the pubic bone in front to the tailbone behind. It forms a supportive sling with openings for the urethra and back passage.

These muscles help with:

- Holding urine and wind
- Support when you cough, sneeze, lift or stand up
- Core stability with the belly and hips
- Support around the prostate

A squeeze lifts and closes. A full release lets the hammock soften again. Both matter: strength without relaxation can leave the muscles tight and less useful.
