Our levels, cues and safety rules are drawn from clinical pelvic floor guidance for men, not from testimonials or invented statistics.

We lean on:

- NHS and Prostate Scotland / NHS Fife materials on pelvic floor exercises before and after prostate surgery (catheter rule, dosing, patience with recovery)
- Clinical education on finding the muscles, slow and quick squeezes, the Knack, and when to relax instead of squeeze
- The product rule that timelines say results vary, and that pain routes you to relaxation first

Sources for each article are listed below that article. We do not claim a physiotherapist endorsement in the app until a named review is complete.
