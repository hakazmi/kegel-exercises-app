After prostate removal, urine control often relies more on the pelvic floor. Leakage at first is common. Results vary; improvement can take months.

Before surgery, practising can help the muscles start strong. After surgery, restart only when your clinical team says so — usually once the catheter is out. Do not do squeezes while a catheter is in place; that can irritate the bladder.

Keep sessions gentle. Do not over-exercise; tired muscles recover more slowly. Use a squeeze just before standing, coughing or lifting when you can. Pads can help while control returns.

Follow your hospital's advice first. See a doctor or continence physiotherapist if leakage stays severe or you are unsure you are squeezing correctly.
