Pelvic floor training is a habit, not a quick fix. Results vary from person to person.

Many men notice change after weeks of steady practice, and fuller change can take months. After prostate surgery, continence often improves gradually over several months. Night control may return before daytime control.

What helps:

- Short sessions done often
- Full relax between squeezes
- Using a squeeze just before a cough, sneeze or stand (the Knack), once you know the muscles

What to avoid: expecting overnight change, or doing far more than the plan. If nothing improves after about eight weeks of consistent work, talk to a doctor. This app never predicts your outcome.
