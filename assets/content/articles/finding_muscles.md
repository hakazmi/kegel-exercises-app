Your pelvic floor is a hammock of muscle from the pubic bone to the tailbone. It supports the bladder and bowel and helps close the urethra.

To find it once: gently stop or slow urine mid-stream, or squeeze as if holding in wind. You should feel a lift inside, not a clamp of the buttocks or thighs. Do not use stopping urine as an exercise.

Then practise without peeing: shorten and lift, hold briefly, and fully let go. Keep breathing. Soften the buttocks, thighs and stomach.
